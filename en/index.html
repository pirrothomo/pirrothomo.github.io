Author

Prof. Dr. Pirro Thomo (born 1939) has devoted his life to the survey, documentation and restoration of cultural monuments in Albania. He is the author of more than twenty books and over sixty scholarly articles published at home and abroad. He was co-author, with E. Riza, of Architecture Traditionnelle des Pays Balkaniques (Éditions Melissa, 1990), and a contributor to the Multilingual Illustrated Dictionary of Byzantine Architecture and Sculpture Terminology (Crete University Press, 2010), edited by Sophia Kalopissi-Verti and Maria Panayotidi-Kesioglou of the University of Athens.

He has led or collaborated on more than thirty restoration projects, including work in the monasteries of Mount Athos and in the historic centre of Thessaloniki. The restoration of the Church of St Panteleimon in Thessaloniki, which he directed, received a Europa Nostra award. As editor-in-chief he oversaw 14 issues of the journal Monumentet, from no. 51/2009–2010 to no. 63/2024 (the journal first appeared in 1971).
