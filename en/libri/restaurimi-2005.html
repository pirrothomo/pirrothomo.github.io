Restoration of the Orthodox Church Monuments in Albania

Pirro Thomo, Gentian (John) Stratobërdha · First edition · 2005

The basic record of the restoration of more than 50 historic church buildings, carried out under the care of the Orthodox Autocephalous Church of Albania after 1991. Each monument is given a historical and architectural account, its condition before the work, the consolidation and restoration carried out, who did it, when, and what it cost, with photographs before and after. The foreword is by Archbishop Anastasios.
